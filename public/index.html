          How your privacy is protected
          
    
            
      
          • Encrypted on your device. Files, names and notes are sealed with XSalsa20‑Poly1305 in your browser before upload.
            • 
      
          • The key never reaches the host. It lives after the # in the link — browsers never send that part to servers.
            • 
      
          • Sizes are blurred. Data is padded to 1 MiB blocks, so even file sizes stay vague.
            • 
      
          • Memory only. The host keeps ciphertext in RAM, never on disk, and wipes it on expiry, revoke or self‑destruct.
            • 
      
          • Unlisted. No one can browse shares. Each has a random 128‑bit address that can't be guessed.
            • 
      
          • Tamper‑proof. Every block is authenticated; any modification is detected and rejected.
            • 
      
          • Live sessions too. Paired devices share a key from the QR code. Messages, device names and files are sealed with it — the host only relays noise.
            • 
      
          • Pairing codes stay secret. The six digits never leave your devices — a PAKE turns them into a strong key, and a wrong guess voids the code.
            • 
      
          • Zero third parties. No CDNs, fonts, analytics or cookies. Everything is served from your machine.
